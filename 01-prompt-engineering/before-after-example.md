# Before / After Prompt Example

## Workplace Scenario

**Role:** Digital Solutions Analyst

**Task:** Communicating a project deadline change to the project team.

---

## Before — Weak Prompt

> Write an email about our project deadline being delayed.

### Initial Result

The prompt may produce a general email, but it does not clearly specify:

- Who the audience is.
- The project deadline and context.
- What action the team needs to take.
- The required tone and level of detail.
- What information should not be invented.

---

## After — Improved Prompt

### C.A.R.E. Prompt

**Context:**  
Our project deadline has been delayed by two weeks, from May 30 to June 13.

**Audience:**  
A six-person project team.

**Request:**  
Write a professional and concise email explaining the schedule change and its impact on the project. Ask team members to update their timelines and confirm any issues by Thursday.

**Expectations:**  
Keep the email clear, professional, concise, and action-oriented. Do not invent any additional information.

---

## Improved Result

**Subject: Project Deadline Update — June 13**

Hi Team,

Our project deadline has been moved from May 30 to June 13, giving us an additional two weeks to complete the project.

Please update your individual timelines based on the revised deadline and confirm any issues or dependencies by Thursday.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Best regards,

Digital Solutions Analyst

---

## What Improved?

- **Clear Context:** The original and revised deadlines are explicitly provided.
- **Defined Audience:** The message is written specifically for the six-person project team.
- **Clear Action:** Team members are asked to update their timelines and report issues.
- **Controlled Output:** The prompt specifies a professional, concise, and action-oriented tone and prevents invented information.
