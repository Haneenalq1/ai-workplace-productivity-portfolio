# Professional Writing Workflow

## Workplace Scenario

**Role:** Digital Solutions Analyst

**Task:** Communicating a project deadline change to the project team.

---

## 1. AI-Generated Draft

**Subject: Project Deadline Update**

Hi Team,

Our project deadline has been delayed by two weeks, from May 30 to June 13.

Please update your timelines based on the new deadline and confirm any issues by Thursday.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Best regards,
Digital Solutions Analyst

---

## 2. Fact Verification

The draft was checked against the provided project information.

- Original deadline: May 30
- New deadline: June 13
- Audience: Six-person project team
- Required action: Update timelines
- Required confirmation: Report any issues by Thursday
- No additional reason for the delay was added.

---

## 3. Tone Refinement

The message was refined to ensure that it is:

- Professional
- Concise
- Neutral
- Clear
- Action-oriented

---

## 4. Human Review and Sign-Off

The final message was reviewed by the trainee to confirm that the information is accurate, the tone is appropriate, and no unsupported information was added.

---

## 5. Final Version

**Subject: Project Deadline Update — June 13**

Hi Team,

Our project deadline has been moved from May 30 to June 13, giving us an additional two weeks to complete the project.

Please update your individual timelines based on the revised deadline and confirm any issues or dependencies by Thursday.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Best regards,

Digital Solutions Analyst

---

## Workflow Summary

**Draft → Verify → Refine → Human Review → Final Version**

This workflow helps ensure that AI-assisted workplace communication is accurate, professional, and reviewed by a human before use.
