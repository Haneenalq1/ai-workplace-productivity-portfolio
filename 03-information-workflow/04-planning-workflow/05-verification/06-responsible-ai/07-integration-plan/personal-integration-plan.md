# Personal Integration Plan

## Workplace Scenario

**Role:** Digital Solutions Analyst

The following plan describes how I can use Generative AI responsibly to improve recurring workplace tasks.

---

## 1. Project Information Summarization

**Recurring Task:** Summarize project updates, meeting notes, and long messages.

**Expected Benefit:**  
Save time and quickly identify important decisions, risks, actions, and open issues.

**Preferred Prompt / Workflow:**  
Use the C.A.R.E. framework to summarize information into Key Decisions, Risks, Actions, Open Issues, and Missing Information.

**Risk / Verification Requirement:**  
Compare important facts, dates, and decisions with the original source. Do not allow AI to invent missing information.

**Frequency:**  
2–3 times per week.

**What Success Looks Like:**  
Project information can be reviewed faster while important details remain accurate and traceable.

---

## 2. Professional Workplace Communication

**Recurring Task:** Draft project updates, emails, and stakeholder follow-ups.

**Expected Benefit:**  
Improve clarity, professionalism, and consistency while reducing the time needed to prepare messages.

**Preferred Prompt / Workflow:**  
Use C.A.R.E. prompts followed by the workflow: Draft → Verify → Refine → Human Review.

**Risk / Verification Requirement:**  
Check names, dates, numbers, and commitments before sending. Review the final tone and content personally.

**Frequency:**  
2–4 times per week.

**What Success Looks Like:**  
Messages are clear, professional, accurate, and require only minor human editing before use.

---

## 3. Project Planning and Organization

**Recurring Task:** Organize project goals, phases, tasks, dependencies, and risks.

**Expected Benefit:**  
Turn broad project goals into clear and actionable plans.

**Preferred Prompt / Workflow:**  
Use the planning chain: Goal → Mechanisms → Phases → Tasks.

**Risk / Verification Requirement:**  
Check that tasks, owners, deadlines, and dependencies are supported by the available information. Use [Not Specified] when information is missing.

**Frequency:**  
Once per week or whenever a new project or major task begins.

**What Success Looks Like:**  
Projects have clearer priorities, organized tasks, visible dependencies, and fewer overlooked risks.

---

## Personal AI Use Principles

- Use AI as a support tool, not as a replacement for human judgment.
- Verify important information against reliable sources.
- Never invent missing information.
- Avoid entering confidential, private, or sensitive information into inappropriate AI tools.
- Review AI-generated content before using or sharing it.
- Keep human accountability for final decisions.

---

## Success Measures

My use of Generative AI will be successful when it:

1. Reduces time spent on repetitive workplace tasks.
2. Improves the clarity and structure of project information.
3. Supports better organization and communication.
4. Maintains accuracy through human verification.
5. Allows me to focus more time on analysis and decision-making.

---

## Integration Summary

**Summarize → Communicate → Plan → Verify → Improve**

I will use Generative AI as a practical productivity assistant while maintaining human review, accountability, and responsible use.
