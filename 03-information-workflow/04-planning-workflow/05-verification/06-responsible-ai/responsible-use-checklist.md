# Responsible AI Use Checklist

## Workplace Scenario

**Role:** Digital Solutions Analyst

Generative AI can support workplace productivity, but its outputs must be used responsibly and reviewed by a human.

---

## 1. Accuracy

AI-generated information should be checked against the original source before it is used. Important facts, dates, numbers, and decisions should be verified.

---

## 2. Accountability

The human user remains responsible for the final content and decisions. AI should support the work, not replace human responsibility.

---

## 3. Confidentiality

Confidential business information, internal project information, or sensitive organizational data should not be entered into public AI tools or uploaded to a public GitHub repository.

---

## 4. Privacy

Personal or sensitive information should be protected. Portfolio examples should use fictional or anonymized information instead of real personal data.

---

## 5. Bias

AI outputs should be reviewed for unfair assumptions, stereotypes, or biased recommendations. Important workplace decisions should not rely on AI output without human judgment.

---

## 6. Human Oversight

AI-generated content should be reviewed by a human before it is used or shared. The human should verify the result and make the final decision.

---

## Green / Amber / Red Classification

| Information Item | Classification | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Fictional project meeting notes used in this portfolio | Green | No real personal, confidential, or sensitive information is included. |
| Anonymized workplace information | Amber | It can be used only after checking that individuals and sensitive details cannot be identified. |
| Real confidential company information or personal data | Red | It should not be entered into public AI tools or uploaded to a public GitHub repository. |

---

## Responsible Use Decision

For this portfolio, only fictional or anonymized examples are used.

No real confidential, private, personal, or sensitive organizational information is included. AI-generated content was reviewed by the trainee before being included.

---

## Responsible AI Summary

**Accuracy → Accountability → Confidentiality → Privacy → Bias → Human Oversight**

These principles help ensure that Generative AI is used safely and responsibly in workplace tasks.
