# Verification Checklist

## Workplace Scenario

**Role:** Digital Solutions Analyst

**Purpose:** Verify AI-generated workplace outputs before they are used or shared.

---

## Personal Verification Checklist

Before using an AI-generated output, I check the following:

1. **Identify the content:** I clearly identify what the AI output is claiming or recommending.

2. **Set the criteria:** I decide what makes the output accurate, relevant, complete, and appropriate for the task.

3. **Check against the source:** I compare important facts, names, dates, numbers, and decisions with the original information.

4. **Check for missing information:** I make sure the AI has not invented owners, deadlines, facts, or other details that were not provided.

5. **Check the meaning:** I confirm that the AI output has not changed the meaning of the original information.

6. **Confirm the result:** I verify that the final output meets the requirements of the workplace task.

7. **Document and decide:** I record important verification findings and decide whether the output is ready to use, needs revision, or should not be used.
