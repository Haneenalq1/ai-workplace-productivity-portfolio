# Planning Workflow

## Workplace Scenario

**Role:** Digital Solutions Analyst

**Task:** Planning a digital product launch when the project team is facing time and resource constraints.

---

## 1. Goal

Prepare a realistic launch plan for the digital product while reducing delivery risks and protecting the most important launch activities.

---

## 2. Mechanisms

The planning approach focuses on:

- Prioritizing critical launch activities.
- Identifying dependencies and risks.
- Organizing work into clear phases.
- Separating confirmed information from assumptions.
- Keeping owners and deadlines as [Not Specified] when they are not provided.

---

## 3. Phases

### Phase 1 — Assess Current Status

Review the current project progress, open issues, resources, and launch requirements.

### Phase 2 — Prioritize Critical Work

Identify tasks that directly affect launch readiness and prioritize them based on impact, effort, time, cost, risk, and possibility.

### Phase 3 — Resolve Dependencies

Address critical technical issues, testing requirements, approvals, and other dependencies before launch.

### Phase 4 — Launch Preparation

Complete final checks, confirm readiness, and prepare the product for release.

---

## 5. Planning Considerations

The plan should be evaluated using:

- **Impact:** How strongly the task affects launch success.
- **Effort:** How much work is required.
- **Time:** How quickly the task can be completed.
- **Cost:** Resources or budget required.
- **Risk:** Potential negative impact if the task is delayed or missed.
- **Possibility:** Whether the task can realistically be completed with available resources.

---

## 6. Human Review

The AI-generated plan was reviewed to ensure that:

- No missing owners or deadlines were invented.
- Dependencies and risks were clearly identified.
- Critical tasks were prioritized.
- Uncertain information remained marked as [Not Specified].
- The plan remained realistic for the available time and resources.

---

## 7. Final Planning Chain

**Goal → Mechanisms → Phases → Tasks**

This structure helps a Digital Solutions Analyst turn a broad project goal into an organized and actionable plan while keeping uncertainty visible.
