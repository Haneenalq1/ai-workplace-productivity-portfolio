# Planning Workflow

## Workplace Scenario

**Role:** Digital Solutions Analyst

**Task:** Planning a digital product launch when the project team is facing time and resource constraints.

---

## 1. Goal

Prepare a realistic launch plan for the digital product while reducing delivery risks and protecting the most important launch activities.

---

## 2. Mechanisms

The planning approach uses:

- Prioritizing critical launch activities.
- Breaking the work into clear phases.
- Identifying dependencies between tasks.
- Highlighting risks and resource constraints.
- Keeping missing information marked as [Not Specified].

---

## 3. Phases

### Phase 1: Preparation

- Confirm the launch scope and key requirements.
- Review available team resources.
- Identify critical dependencies.
- Confirm the most important launch activities.

### Phase 2: Development and Testing

- Complete the highest-priority product work.
- Conduct functional testing.
- Resolve critical issues.
- Allow 1–2 days for fixes and testing where needed.

### Phase 3: Launch Readiness

- Complete final checks.
- Confirm communication materials.
- Review unresolved risks.
- Confirm launch readiness with the relevant stakeholders.

### Phase 4: Launch and Follow-up

- Launch the digital product.
- Monitor initial issues and user feedback.
- Record lessons learned.
- Plan follow-up improvements.

## 5. Dependencies and Risks

### Key Dependencies

- Confirmed project scope.
- Availability of required team members.
- Completion of priority development work.
- Testing results before launch.
- Final readiness approval.

### Key Risks

- Limited team capacity.
- Delays in development or testing.
- Critical issues discovered late.
- Missing stakeholder decisions.
- [Not Specified] information affecting the launch timeline.

### Risk Response

Prioritize critical work, review dependencies regularly, verify important information, and escalate unresolved issues before the launch decision.

---

## Human Review

The AI-generated plan should be reviewed by a human before use. The reviewer should confirm that:

- Tasks match the actual project.
- Dependencies are realistic.
- No unsupported deadlines or owners were invented.
- Missing information is clearly marked as [Not Specified].
- Risks and priorities reflect the actual project situation.

---

## Final Planning Output

The final plan provides a structured sequence from preparation to launch and follow-up. It focuses on critical activities, dependencies, risks, and human verification while avoiding unsupported assumptions.
