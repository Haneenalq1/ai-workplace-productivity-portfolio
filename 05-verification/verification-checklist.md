# Verification Checklist

## Workplace Scenario

**Role:** Digital Solutions Analyst

**Purpose:** Verify AI-generated workplace outputs before they are used or shared.

---

## Personal Verification Checklist

Before using an AI-generated output, I check the following:

1. **Identify the task:** Confirm that the output addresses the requested task and audience.

2. **Set criteria:** Check that the output follows the required format, tone, and instructions.

3. **Check the source:** Verify factual claims against reliable or approved sources when needed.

4. **Check missing information:** Make sure AI has not invented details. Use `[Not Specified]` when information is unavailable.

5. **Check meaning:** Confirm that the output preserves the intended meaning and does not introduce misleading assumptions.

6. **Confirm the result:** Review the final output for accuracy, completeness, clarity, and professionalism.

7. **Document and decide:** Decide whether the output is ready to use, needs revision, or requires additional verification.

---

## Applied Example

### Output Reviewed
Professional project update email about a project deadline change.

### Verification Results

- **Task:** Confirmed that the email explains the deadline change and requested actions.
- **Criteria:** Professional tone and concise structure were maintained.
- **Facts:** The dates and team size were checked against the provided project information.
- **Missing information:** No unsupported reason for the delay was added.
- **Meaning:** The message preserved the intended communication.
- **Final review:** The email was clear and appropriate for the project team.
- **Decision:** Approved after human review.

## Human Sign-Off

AI can assist with drafting and checking, but I remain responsible for verifying the final output before using or sharing it.
