# Responsible AI Use Checklist

## Workplace Scenario

**Role:** Digital Solutions Analyst

**Purpose:** Use AI responsibly while protecting accuracy, privacy, and human accountability.

## Responsible AI Checklist

### 1. Accuracy
Verify important AI-generated information before using or sharing it.

### 2. Accountability
A human remains responsible for the final decision and output.

### 3. Confidentiality
Do not enter confidential company information, credentials, or sensitive project data into AI tools.

### 4. Privacy
Avoid using personal or identifying information unless it is necessary and appropriately protected.

### 5. Bias
Review outputs for unfair assumptions, stereotypes, or biased recommendations.

### 6. Human Oversight
Use human review for important decisions and final approval.

## Risk Classification

| Level | Example | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Drafting a general project email | AI can assist with normal review |
| Amber | Summarizing project information for stakeholders | Verify facts and review before sharing |
| Red | Making high-impact decisions about people | Do not rely on AI alone; require human judgment |

## Final Statement

AI should support workplace productivity, not replace human responsibility. I will verify important outputs and use appropriate safeguards before using or sharing AI-generated content.
