# Personal Integration Plan

## Workplace Scenario

**Role:** Digital Solutions Analyst

**Purpose:** Integrate AI into recurring workplace tasks while maintaining human review and responsible use.

## 1. Project Information Summarization

**Task:** Summarize meeting notes and project updates into clear action items and decisions.

**Expected Benefit:** Save time and make project information easier to review.

**Preferred Workflow:** Information Workflow with structured output.

**Risk and Verification:** AI may miss or misinterpret details, so I will compare the summary with the original notes.

**Frequency:** After important meetings or project updates.

**Success Criteria:** Clear, accurate summaries with decisions, actions, and missing information identified.

---

## 2. Professional Communication

**Task:** Draft and refine professional emails and project updates.

**Expected Benefit:** Improve clarity, tone, and consistency while reducing drafting time.

**Preferred Workflow:** Writing Workflow and Prompt Library.

**Risk and Verification:** AI may add unsupported information or use an inappropriate tone, so I will verify facts and review the final message.

**Frequency:** As needed for project communication.

**Success Criteria:** Professional, accurate, concise messages that require minimal editing.

---

## 3. Project Planning and Organization

**Task:** Break project goals into phases, tasks, dependencies, and risks.

**Expected Benefit:** Improve organization and make project priorities easier to manage.

**Preferred Workflow:** Planning Workflow.

**Risk and Verification:** AI may create unrealistic tasks or assumptions, so I will review dependencies, timelines, and missing information.

**Frequency:** At the start of a project and when plans change.

**Success Criteria:** A practical plan with clear tasks, dependencies, risks, and priorities.

---

## Responsible Use

I will use AI as a productivity assistant while maintaining human oversight. I will verify important information, avoid entering confidential or sensitive data, and remain responsible for final decisions and outputs.
